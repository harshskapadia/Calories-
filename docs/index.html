              How it works & credits
              • Dish model: ConvNeXt-Tiny fine-tuned on Food-101 + an Indian food dataset (139 dishes), 88.8% top-1 / 97.7% top-5 on 26,836 test photos; int8 ONNX (34 MB) running in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web, so no photo is uploaded.
              • Identify anything: CLIP (open_clip ViT-B/32, DataComp-XL) against every USDA food plus world dishes from Wikidata; world-dish nutrition is approximate (borrowed from the most similar USDA food).
              • Whole plate: Ultralytics YOLO26 instance segmentation (AGPL-3.0) trained on FoodSeg103 (103 foods and ingredients) outlines each food; its portion is a rough guess from the share of the photo it covers.
              • Calories from the photo: trained on MM-Food-100K (Humanbased-AI, OpenRAIL) real meal photos, whose calories are GPT-4o/Qwen estimates reviewed by people.
              • Confidence is calibrated, so "70%" means roughly 70% correct; below 40% the app says it isn't sure.

              Training data: Food-101 (Bossard et al., 2014; research use) and SohlHealth Enhanced Indian Food Classification (CC-BY-4.0). FoodSeg103 (Wu et al., 2021; Apache-2.0). Runtime: ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Barcodes: zxing-wasm (MIT). Packaged products: Open Food Facts contributors, under the ODbL.
