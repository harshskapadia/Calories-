        How it works & credits
        
                            
          
                          • Dish model: ConvNeXt-Tiny fine-tuned on Food-101, two Indian food datasets, Central Asian Food Scenes, Food Recognition 2022, TurkishFoods-25, MM-Food-100K phone photos and Wikimedia Commons photos of world dishes (425 dishes, drinks and fruits), with the previous model as a helper: 87.4% top-1
            on the 228 long-standing dishes, ~52% on the newer world dishes; int8 ONNX (2 × 34 MB) running in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web, so no photo is uploaded.
                            • 
          
                          • Identify anything: CLIP (open_clip ViT-B/32, DataComp-XL) against every USDA food plus world dishes from Wikidata;
            world-dish nutrition is approximate (borrowed from the most similar USDA food).
                            • 
          
                          • Whole plate: Ultralytics YOLO26 instance segmentation (AGPL-3.0) trained on FoodSeg103 (103 foods and
            ingredients) outlines each food; its portion is a rough guess from the share of the photo it covers.
                            • 
          
                          • Calories from the photo: trained on MM-Food-100K (Humanbased-AI, OpenRAIL) real meal photos, whose calories are
            GPT-4o/Qwen estimates reviewed by people.
                            • 
          
                          • Confidence is calibrated, so "70%" means roughly 70% correct; below 40% the app says it isn't sure.
                            • 
        
                          
        
                          Training data: Food-101 (Bossard et al., 2014; research use) and SohlHealth Enhanced Indian Food Classification (CC-BY-4.0).
          FoodSeg103 (Wu et al., 2021; Apache-2.0). Runtime: ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Barcodes: zxing-wasm (MIT). Packaged products:
          Open Food Facts contributors, under the
          ODbL.
