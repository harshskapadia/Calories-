NutriSnap

Privacy

Short version: your photos and your data stay on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no ads.

What happens on your phone

  • Photos are analysed by AI models that run inside your browser. They are never uploaded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your calorie target, food log, weight history and learned corrections are saved only in this browser's storage on this device. "Learns from you" keeps a numeric fingerprint of a corrected photo (a list of 768 numbers), not the photo.
  • We can't see any of it, and we can't recover it for you. Use My day → Backup to keep a copy.

What uses the internet

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
Opening the appNormal web requests to download the app and its AI models (no personal data) GitHub Pages, which hosts the files and, like every web host, sees your IP address
📦 Packaged food (barcode / product search)The barcode number or the words you search for Open Food Facts
🎤 Voice input (optional)Your voice, if you tap the microphone Your browser's own speech service (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari)

Nothing else leaves your device. Meal photos are never sent, not even for packaged food.

Your control

  • Backup / restore: My day → 💾 Backup & privacy → Download backup (a file you keep), or Restore from file.
  • Forget corrections: My day → 🧠 Learned from you → Forget all.
  • Delete everything: My day → 💾 Backup & privacy → Delete all my data. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings does the same.

Health information

Calorie and nutrition values are estimates, not medical advice. The daily target uses a standard formula with safety limits; if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, have an eating disorder or a medical condition, talk to a doctor or dietitian first.

NutriSnap is open source (AGPL-3.0): source code on GitHub. Questions or concerns can be raised there as an issue. Last updated: November 2026.
