          How it works

          • Model: ConvNeXt-Tiny fine-tuned on Food-101 + an Indian food dataset (139 dishes), 88.8% top-1 / 97.7% top-5 on 26,836 test photos. For anything else, Identify anything uses CLIP (open_clip ViT-B/32, DataComp-XL) against a large list of food names (every USDA food, plus world dishes from Wikidata when built; their nutrition is approximate, borrowed from the most similar USDA food). Exported to ONNX with int8 weights (34 MB) and run in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web, so no photo is uploaded anywhere.
          • Confidence is calibrated (temperature scaling) so "70%" means roughly 70% correct; below 40% the app says it's not sure.
          • Nutrition: USDA FoodData Central (FNDDS) per-100 g values × your portion; typical servings from USDA portion data.

          Source code, training pipeline, evaluation and the full Gradio app (with Grad-CAM heatmaps): github.com/harshskapadia/Calories-

          Training data: Food-101 (Bossard et al., 2014; research use) and the SohlHealth Enhanced Indian Food Classification dataset (CC-BY-4.0). Runtime: ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Barcodes: zxing-wasm (MIT). Packaged products: Open Food Facts contributors, database under the ODbL.
